Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Munch Spin (Android, package com.ctknight.munchspin), published by Cagri Tekmen (CT Knight), handles your data.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the game, this data is deleted. We do not run our own servers for the game and do not receive your progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional reward videos (for an extra Magic Spin) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. AdMob may use device identifiers and coarse location to show and measure ads. This is done by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Your consent and choices

In the EEA and the UK, the game asks for your consent with Google's UMP form before ads are requested. You can accept, refuse, or manage options. You can open the form again in the game: Settings → Privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

In some US states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for ads through the same form: Settings → Privacy choices.

Analytics

The game does not include an analytics SDK. We do not track how you play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and the consent form, and vibration for game feedback (you can turn vibration off in Settings). It does not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date above.
